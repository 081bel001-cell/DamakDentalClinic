Who Needs Professional Cleaning?

  • Visible tartar near gums or behind front teeth
  • Gum bleeding during brushing or flossing
  • Persistent bad breath despite home care
  • Tea/coffee/tobacco staining on teeth
  • More than 6 months since last cleaning

Cleaning FAQs

How long does dental cleaning take?

Most appointments finish in 30 to 45 minutes depending on tartar level.

Is scaling painful?

Most patients feel mild pressure only. We adjust technique for sensitive gums.

How often should I do cleaning?

Every 6 months is ideal for most adults, with shorter intervals for gum issues.
